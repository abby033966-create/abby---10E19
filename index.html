Sam Smith

Unshakable Authenticity

Why Sam Smith inspires me: an English singer-songwriter who built a global career while staying true to who they are.

When we think of modern music icons, we look for individuals who combine incredible creative talent with real-life courage. English singer-songwriter Sam Smith stands out as a powerful example of both. Famous around the world for their emotional vocals and hit songs, Sam has built a global career while staying true to who they are.

Key life milestones

  1. 1992–2012

    Early dedication

    Born in London in 1992, Sam spent years studying jazz singing, participating in youth choirs, and working regular jobs before finding their breakthrough in the music industry.

  2. 2012–2014

    Global breakthrough

    After featuring on Disclosure’s single “Latch” (2012), Sam released their debut album, In the Lonely Hour (2014). The record launched them into international stardom and won four Grammy Awards, including Best New Artist and Record of the Year.

  3. 2015–2023

    Historic recognition

    In 2015, Sam won an Academy Award and a Golden Globe for the James Bond theme song “Writing’s on the Wall”. Later in 2023, Sam made history by winning a Grammy alongside Kim Petras for their chart-topping hit “Unholy”.

Iconic music

Sam Smith’s discography is known for its deep vulnerability and vocal range.

  • “Stay with Me”

    Soulful ballad

    A breakout soulful ballad about heartbreak that connected with listeners worldwide.

  • “Too Good at Goodbyes”

    Emotional self-defense

    An honest track exploring emotional self-defense after painful relationships.

  • “Unholy” (feat. Kim Petras)

    Bold pop

    A bold pop track showcasing Sam’s artistic growth and boundary-pushing style.

Inspiring actions and advocacy

Resilience against scrutiny

Coming out publicly as non-binary in the mainstream pop industry brought intense public pressure and media criticism. Instead of changing their image to satisfy critics, Sam responded with self-assurance, showing young people that true strength comes from accepting yourself.

Creating a safe platform for youth

Sam regularly uses their social media, concerts, and public interviews to encourage young people to embrace their gender expression and identity without fear.

Community support

Through humanitarian effort and charity partnerships, Sam actively supports organizations focused on LGBTQ+ youth support, homelessness, and mental health awareness.

“If people want to be wrong about who and what I am, that’s egg on their face, not mine. I know at the end of the day who I am.”

Sam Smith

Why Sam Smith inspires me

Sam Smith is an inspirational symbol to me because they prove that success does not require hiding who you are. In a world that often demands people to fit into rigid boxes, Sam chooses to live authentically.

Their ability to transform personal struggles into art, and then use that success to lift up others, shows true resilience and integrity. Sam teaches us that standing tall in your own identity is the most powerful thing you can do.
